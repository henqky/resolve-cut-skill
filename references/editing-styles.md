# Reels Editing Styles

Pick a style first; it sets the cut rate, the caption treatment, and the sound.

| Style | Core idea | Cut rate (practitioner default) | Sound | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Talking head** | Speaker on camera, B-roll for context | A reframe or zoom every few seconds | Voice-first; music low under speech | Easy to produce. Jump cuts between sentence chunks. |
| **Vlog** | Immediate experience is the value | Medium, follows real time | Natural ambience; light music | Direct vlog often beats an over-scripted treatment. |
| **Cinematic / narrative** | Immersive, story-driven, intentional | Slower; hold on images | Score-led, sound design, ducked VO | Needs more time and footage. Less common in short-form. |
| **Documentary (micro)** | Subject and a central question | Medium; beats per question | Interview VO + ambience; ducked music | 9:16 framing alone does not make it documentary. |
| **Meme / hype** | Fast, joke-driven, trend format | Fast; cut on the beat | Loud music; SFX on hits | Trends are short-lived. Crop landscape memes so one subject carries the joke. |
| **Beat-synced montage** | Visuals locked to music | Cuts on beats (e.g. every 1 or 2 bars) | Music is the timeline | Zoom in to check each cut lands on the beat. |

Sources: [Opus – 11 editing aesthetics 2026](https://www.opus.pro/blog/editing-aesthetics-dominating-short-form-2026),
[Skedsocial – 11 short-form styles](https://skedsocial.com/blog/short-form-video-styles),
[CapCut – vertical meme editing](https://www.capcut.com/create/vertical-video-meme-editing-mobile-first-platforms),
[CapCut – micro-documentary](https://www.capcut.com/create/micro-documentary-short-form-video).

**Gap:** the sources do not give measured seconds-per-cut per style. Measure a sample of top
reels in your niche before committing to a number.
